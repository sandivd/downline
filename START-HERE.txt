DOWNLINE
Your local video downloader

1. Extract the entire ZIP first.
   Put the Downline folder somewhere like Documents.

2. Open the Downline folder.
   Double-click: Start Downline.cmd
   It opens your default web browser.

3. The first launch downloads the tools it needs.
   Leave the setup window open. It can take a few minutes.
   You do not need to install Python or type commands.

4. When the app opens:
   - Paste a YouTube, Pornhub, Instagram, TikTok, X/Twitter,
     or Twitch video link.
   - Pick MP4 video, MKV video, or MP3 audio.
   - Pick your preferred quality.
   - Click Add to download queue.

Files go to Downloads > Downline.
Change the folder in Settings.
The sun/moon switch changes light and dark mode.
The power button at the bottom left quits the app.

Next time, double-click Start Downline.cmd again.

UPDATING AN EXISTING INSTALLATION

Quit Downline using its power button first.
Copy the updated files into your existing Downline folder and choose Replace.
Keep your existing runtime folder. Settings and history stay on your PC.
Then run Start Downline.cmd again.

Twitch: use a clip or past broadcast, not a live channel.
For posts with several videos, the first video is saved.
Instagram and some restricted posts may need your own cookies.txt in Settings.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 and an internet connection.
Some site-restricted videos may not be downloadable.

For more detail and troubleshooting, open README.md.

# Platform logo sources

YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X, and Twitch SVG paths are from [Simple Icons 14.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/14.15.0), distributed under CC0-1.0. The original paths are embedded directly in the interface. Colours follow each brand, with neutral marks adapting to the theme and a lighter Twitch purple in dark mode.

Source pages recorded by Simple Icons:

- [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/resources/brand-resources/#logos-icons-and-colors)
- [Instagram](https://about.meta.com/brand/resources/instagram)
- [TikTok](https://tiktok.com)
- [X](https://about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit)
- [Twitch](https://brand.twitch.tv)

The Pornhub wordmark is from [Wikimedia Commons: Pornhub-logo.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pornhub-logo.svg), credited there to Pornhub and marked as a public-domain text logo. Its original vector shapes, colours, and aspect ratio are preserved.

Platform names and logos remain the trademarks of their respective owners. Logos identify the supported sources; they do not indicate affiliation or endorsement. The app loads these bundled logos locally, without external image requests.
